Three UNT Librarians Awarded FY2027 Research Buyouts

July 2026

The University of North Texas Libraries selected three Level 3 PDI Fellows—Ana Krahmer, Maristella Feustle, and William Hicks—for one-month research buyouts between September 2026 and June 2027. Their research projects focus on sustaining state and national digital newspaper preservation programs, building geolocated historical and socio-economic research databases, and creating immersive visual archival recordings of culturally historic sites in preparation for the Texas 2036 Bicentennial.

CU Boulder Awards Follow-On Funding to Collaborative Research Teams

July 2026

University Libraries at CU Boulder awarded Level 3 follow-on funding to its four collaborative research teams to further advance their projects through June 2027. The additional support covers student assistants, data gathering, interview incentives, conference travel, and publication fees to produce scholarly deliverables including manuscripts, presentations, and datasets.

Virginia Tech University Libraries completed the selection of four Level 3 PDI Fellows across two rounds in April and July 2026, providing dedicated research time to pursue focused scholarly work and collaborative research initiatives.

UNT Libraries Awards Six Collaborative Research Grants

June 2026

The University of North Texas Libraries selected six Level 2 projects for FY2027 funding under the University Libraries Research and Innovation Grants (ULRIG) program. Led by Morgan Gieringer, Amanda Zerangue, Chassidy Miles, Christa Hiemann, Meranda Roy, and Tracy Brewster, the funded projects explore a library book lab feasibility study, AI literacy grounded theory analysis, engineering practice programs, music library needs analysis, AI-supported OER development, and open access workflow agility.

Joint PDI Project Launches

August 1, 2023

The Joint Professional Development Institute (Joint PDI) to Cultivate Collaborative Library Scholars project launches with support from the IMLS (RE-254915-OLS-23). This collaborative effort between Virginia Tech, University of North Texas, University of Colorado Boulder, Los Alamos National Lab, and University of California Riverside aims to support and champion active, interdisciplinary research collaborations among academic and research library staff.
